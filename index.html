The residence

A rare plan,
on the water.

The Savannah is the plan people wait for in Bella Vita. This one sits on the pond with 2,908 heated square feet, a third garage bay, and a fenced lawn that opens directly onto the water.

Daily life happens entirely on the main level — the primary suite, two further bedrooms, a dedicated study, and an open kitchen and living room beneath a vaulted ceiling. The staircase is optional rather than routine.

Above it, a second world: a private bedroom with full bath opening onto a generous loft, with deep storage and oversized closets. A second primary in all but name.
