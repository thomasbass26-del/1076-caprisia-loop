The home

The Savannah plan,
finally available.

This is the floor plan buyers wait for in Bella Vita. Beazer's Savannah puts the majority of the living space on the main level — so daily life happens on one floor — and reserves the upstairs for a private suite and loft that look out over the lake.

That upper level is what sets this house apart. It works as a guest suite, a multigenerational wing, a home office with a view, or simply the quiet end of the house. A third-car garage handles the boat, the golf cart, or the workshop.

Bella Vita is an all-natural-gas Beazer community on the edge of Carolina Forest, minutes from Highway 31 and about fifteen from the ocean.
