The home

A rare plan,
on the water.

The Savannah is the plan people wait for in Bella Vita, and this one is under seven years old with 2,908 heated square feet, a three-car garage, and a fenced yard that opens straight onto the lake.

Everything you need sits on the main level — the primary suite, two guest bedrooms, a dedicated office or flex room, a guest bath, powder room, laundry, and an open kitchen and living area under a vaulted ceiling. Upstairs is its own world: a large loft, deep storage, oversized closets, and a private bedroom with a full bath that works as a second primary suite.

It is bright, it is genuinely flexible, and it backs up to water in one of Carolina Forest's most established communities.
